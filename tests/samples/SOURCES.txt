Coverage notes
--------------
- All 11 extensions from Filter_scripts/README.md are represented:
  .jpg, .jpeg (Canon_40D.jpg IS a .jpg; no separate .jpeg sample included),
  .jpe, .tif/.tiff, .png, .gif, .bmp, .webp, .heic, .heif.
- Files 1-5 and 10-12 carry real embedded EXIF DateTimeOriginal data (dates
  for grouping); files 6-9 have none, to exercise the no_exif fallback path.
- Capture dates present, for reference:
    2008-03-15  Nikon_D70.jpe
    2008-05-04  Pentax_K10D.jpg
    2008-05-30  Canon_40D.jpg
    2008-07-31  Fujifilm_FinePix_E500.jpg
    2008-11-01  DSCN0010.jpg, DSCN0021.jpg, DSCN0029.jpg, DSCN0042.jpg
      (4 photos, same day, different times — good for testing multi-photo
      grouping under a single date)
