Coverage notes
--------------
- All 11 extensions from Filter_scripts/README.md are represented:
  .jpg, .jpeg (Canon_40D.jpg IS a .jpg; no separate .jpeg sample included),
  .jpe, .tif/.tiff, .png, .gif, .bmp, .webp, .heic, .heif.
- Files 1-5 carry real embedded EXIF data (dates for grouping); files 6-9
  have none, to exercise the no_exif fallback path.
